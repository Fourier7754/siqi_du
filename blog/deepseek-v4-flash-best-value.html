DeepSeek v4 Flash (Official): Best Value on LiveCuratorBench

2026-08-01

On LiveCuratorBench, my closed agentic benchmark for meta-knowledge extraction, the official version of DeepSeek v4 Flash scored 19.33/22 over the official endpoint and 19.00/22 over opencode go—second only to Grok 4.5 (19.67)—at $0.027 per run, about a tenth of Grok's cost. That makes it the best performance per dollar in the thirteen-model field. The official version is substantially stronger than the earlier preview, and the gap between the two channels (0.33 points) is within single-run noise.

Task description, evaluation criteria, and the full thirteen-model results are on the LiveCuratorBench main page.
